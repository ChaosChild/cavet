▸ What changed from the live dashboard, and why — 18 items
AreaObserved on the live pageChange in this mock
Shell width Capped at 72rem. On the 2552px viewport it was reviewed on, content used 1152px and left 55% of the screen empty — while the findings table truncated 17 of 48 cells. Fluid to min(1680px, 94vw). The columns that were truncating get the space.
Stat cards Four cards, 165px tall, one number each; medium, low and info collapsed into a single bucket. One strip at a third the height, all five severities separate and colour-keyed.
Info cards Baseline debt, last scan and scanners each held a full card for one short string. Folded into a meta chip row under the title — run metadata, not metrics.
Verdict flow card 183px of dead space below the canvas: the card stretched to match its taller neighbour while the chart stayed pinned at 170px. Card height follows content, and the remediation panel beside it now fills its own height with real rows.
Verdict flow data 38 weekly buckets rendered, 36 of them empty. Leading empty buckets dropped; 12 weeks shown.
Remediation donuts One donut was a single 100% ring — with one actor it can only ever say "all of it". The other read – and its caption "agent faster" was overlapped by the ring itself. Ranked bars plus a key/value block: the same two facts, plus counts, the multiple, and nothing clipped.
Time to verdict Both series on one axis. A lone 312h triage point forced the domain to 350 and flattened every other point onto the zero line. Two charts, independent domains capped at p90; out-of-range points pin to the cap and carry their real value as a label.
Rule column Ellipsised from the right, so every row read opt.opengrep-rules… — identical, when the distinguishing part is the tail. Rule name on its own line; the namespace is a dim prefix that ellipsises from the left.
Severity cell Badge clipped to medium… inside an 80px column. Full-word pill, severity-coloured, plus a 2px rail down the row.
Location cell Ellipsised from the right, hiding the filename and line — the part you navigate by. Ellipsises from the left, so …/CommentsRail.tsx:51 survives.
Verdict cell One line, cut mid-sentence. Two-line clamp, full text in the panel. Untriaged findings say awaiting triage instead of showing blank.
Empty state The default filter matches nothing on a clean repo: a bare header row and "0 findings · server-side". A real empty state with the triaged counts and a one-click switch to the full list. Pick "open + confirmed" or "nothing" in the status filter above to see it.
Panel width 384px. The header's flex-wrap broke, dropping the close button onto its own line under a 3-line rule id — a 115px header row. 620px, header on a two-column grid so the close button cannot wrap.
Panel title The finding id hash (4ED8FF) was the headline. The rule name is the headline; the id is a mono tag beside the severity.
Panel header Static — severity and the close button scrolled away on long findings. Sticky.
Panel verdict Rationale, confidence, actor and timestamp ran together in one 11px paragraph. Rationale as prose; confidence, actor and stamp as tags beneath it.
Scroll lock body stayed overflow:visible with the panel open, so the page scrolled behind it. Locked while open; Esc and veil-click close, and focus returns to the row.
Contrast Secondary copy at opacity .4 / .5 measures 3.32:1 and 4.41:1 on the card surface, at 10.5–11px. Both fail 4.5:1. Three fixed greys at 6.6:1, 4.8:1, and a 3.2:1 tier reserved for non-text (rules, ticks). Body copy floor raised to 12px.
